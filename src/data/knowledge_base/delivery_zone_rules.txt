URBAN ZONE TRANSIT AND ACCESS REGULATIONS (MUNICIPAL DIRECTIVE 2026)
1. ZONE A (CENTRAL BUSINESS DISTRICT & RESIDENTIAL)
   - Electric vehicles (electric vans and electric scooters) are preferred between 09:00 and 19:00. A non-electric vehicle in this window is allowed but flagged for dispatcher review.
   - Trucks carrying more than 150 kg may not enter Zone A at any time (narrow residential avenues).
   - Local streets inside every zone are planned at 25 km/h.
2. ZONE B (TECH CORRIDOR & COMMERCIAL MALLS)
   - Freight over 50.0 kg may not be picked up in or delivered to Zone B between 17:00 and 20:00 (peak congestion window).
3. ZONE C (UNIVERSITY & CIVIC HUB)
   - No vehicle or time restrictions. Electric delivery vehicles are welcome at all hours.
4. ZONE D (AIRPORT CARGO & LOGISTICS PARK)
   - Trucks are permitted 24 hours a day.
   - Heavy rain: when rainfall in Zone D exceeds 10 mm, roads into and out of Zone D are planned at 15 km/h below their normal speed.
