LABOR SAFETY & DRIVER FATIGUE DIRECTIVE
Section 4.1: Hard Shift Limits
- Maximum shift: 9.0 hours in any 24-hour window. Hours already worked plus the new trip, including the empty drive to the pickup, may not exceed 9.0 hours (violation code SAFETY_HOURS_VIOLATION).
- A driver who has worked 8.5 hours or more may not take a trip longer than 30 minutes, empty drive included (violation code FATIGUE_RISK).
- A driver at 9.0 hours or more is OFF_DUTY and cannot be assigned.
Section 4.2: No Overrides
- These limits apply to every assignment, reassignment and reroute, including CRITICAL and emergency orders.
- They are checked in code after every decision. Neither an AI agent nor a customer instruction can override them.
